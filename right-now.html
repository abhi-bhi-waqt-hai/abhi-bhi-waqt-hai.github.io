What is tonight like?

    1 / 4

    Pick one. Three or four cards, about a minute. Each one lights a lamp.

    Make it safer

    Don’t mix.

    That includes ordinary medicine from a chemist, and most of all anything that makes you sleepy on top of something else that does.

    Breathing

    Tap the things that make you sleepy and watch the breath.

    A picture of the idea, not a measurement.

    World Health Organization

    Without the danger

    Less is a real win.

    Some days instead of most days is a real change in your life. It counts, even if you never get to zero.

    MTWTFSS

    104 fewer days a year.

    Without the danger

    Make the cut small.

    The cut that holds is smaller and more boring than the one you announce. One week done beats a month planned.

    Without the danger

    Don’t stop everything at once.

    For some kinds of dependence, stopping suddenly is the dangerous part, and it can need emergency care. Ask a doctor first, or the helpline. No name needed.

    Ask 14446 → World Health Organization

    Make it safer · Without the danger

    After a break, the old amount is too much.

    Weeks off, an illness, a stay somewhere: your body can’t take what it used to. Starting again after a gap is one of the things that most often goes wrong.

    World Health Organization

    Make it safer

    Tell one person where you are.

    Not a confession, and not a conversation. Just someone who could come and find you.

    Make it safer

    Know 112 by heart.

    Learn it now, while nothing is happening. Nobody looks up a number well at three in the morning.

    112

    Look once, then type it from memory. Nothing dials.

    Nothing to decide

    There is nothing you have to do tonight.

    You don’t have to phone anybody, or tell anybody. You are not behind. If you can sleep, sleep.

    Nothing to decide

    If an urge comes, ride it out.

    An urge rises, peaks and passes. Breathe with the light for ten minutes and watch it get smaller.

    Press and hold. Let go to breathe out.
