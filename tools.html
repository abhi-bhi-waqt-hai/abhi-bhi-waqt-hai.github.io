Take it with you.

Every page, the self-check, the cravings timer and your Reboot log work with no internet. Only the AI (the counsellor, the read-out and the coach) needs a connection.

Android

Download for Android .apk
  1. Tap download, then open the file.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Open “अभी भी वक़्त है” from your home screen.

Windows

Download for Windows .exe
  1. Download and double-click it. Nothing to install.
  2. If Windows warns you, click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  3. Windows 10 or 11.

Help tools.

Check in with yourself, ride out an urge, or find the nearest help. Nothing you do on this page is saved anywhere.

01 · Self-check

A private self-check.

The eleven signs doctors look for, plus a few questions about you. Three minutes. At the end an AI reads your answers, writes you a short personal read-out and sets up your Reboot log. Nothing is saved except that log, on this device.

02 · Cravings timer

An urge is a wave. Ride it out.

Cravings rise, peak and pass, usually within minutes, if you don’t feed them. Start the timer, breathe with the light, and watch the urge get smaller.

03 · Help near me

The nearest help, from where you are.

Hospitals, de-addiction and mental-health places around you, nearest first. Your location stays in this browser; only the point is sent to the public map service to search around it. Always call before you go.
